About CampusTutor

A campus-based peer learning platform connecting students with senior tutors who have already mastered the courses they're taking.

Our Mission

To improve academic performance by building a community where students learn directly from peers who have already mastered the subjects they're studying — making support more accessible, relatable, and effective.

Benefits for Students 🧑‍🎓

  • Learn from seniors who have already passed your courses
  • Get simple, practical, student-to-student explanations
  • Improve your grades faster with personalised support
  • Build confidence in difficult subjects
  • Access support at affordable, student-friendly rates

Benefits for Tutors 👨‍🏫

  • Strengthen your understanding by teaching others
  • Develop communication and leadership skills
  • Build valuable mentoring experience for your CV
  • Earn a small income while you study
  • Grow your academic and professional profile

Who Is It For?

CampusTutor is designed for university students struggling with certain subjects and needing extra support — and for senior students who want to share their knowledge, develop teaching skills, and earn while they study.

How It Works

  1. Search for a tutor by subject
  2. View profiles and reviews
  3. Book a session
  4. Learn and improve
  5. Leave a review after your session

Our Vision

"To create a strong academic community where students support each other, grow together, and make learning easier, more accessible, and more human." 🌍
